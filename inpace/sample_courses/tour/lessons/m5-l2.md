# What's next

You've seen every kind of lesson. To finish the tour:

1. Do the three tasks below this module.
2. Save a **milestone** (Milestones → Save).
3. Send it to someone you trust from **People**.

Then add a course of your own from **Add a course**: a folder of class material, a PDF book, some files, a course file, the course library, or with AI.

**Want a nudge?** Open **Reminders** and pick a time. Tempo will remind you.
